Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026. Published by Hussain Nasir Khan.

In short

Learn MLOps - AI Deploy Lab has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. Your learning progress stays on your device. One outside service is used: Google AdMob shows ads to free users, as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress, XP, streaks, badges, bookmarks and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The name you type for your certificate is saved only on your device.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics of your own learning activity.
  • No selling of personal data.

Ads

Free users see ads from Google AdMob. Buying the full course removes all ads.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address), device and app details, and how you interact with ads.

  • In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first. You can change your choice at any time in Profile → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone and iPad you are asked whether the app may use your advertising ID for tracking. If you choose not to allow it, you still see ads, but they are less personalised.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

See how Google uses this data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The app never sees your payment details. It only receives a confirmation that the course was unlocked.

Children

The lessons are suitable for all ages, but because the free version shows ads, the app is meant for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here in the app.
